06 / Questions

Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The nightly rate includes power, Wi-Fi, housekeeping, security, parking, and access to all amenities. The only extras are optional add-ons you request separately.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer or cash. Full payment confirms your reservation, and a receipt is issued for every payment.

Can I get a discount for a longer stay?

Yes. Stays of 7 nights or more qualify for our reduced Long Stay rate of ₦135,000 per night. Contact our team for a custom quote on extended bookings.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a charge. Please confirm the terms for your dates with our team.

Do rates change during peak periods?

Rates may vary during public holidays and peak periods. Please confirm the rate for your specific dates when you enquire.

How many guests are included?

The standard rate covers up to 2 guests. Additional guests may be accommodated on request — please ask our team for availability and pricing.
