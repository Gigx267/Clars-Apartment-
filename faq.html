02 / Staying

About Your Stay

What time are check-in and check-out?

Standard check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can often be arranged — just mention it when you enquire.

How many guests can stay?

The standard rate covers up to 2 guests. Additional guests may be accommodated on request, subject to availability — please ask our team.

Is parking available?

Yes. Private on-site parking is available inside the gated compound at no extra cost.

Is the apartment suitable for children?

Yes, children are welcome. Please let us know the ages of any young guests when you book so we can prepare accordingly.

Can I cook in the apartment?

Absolutely. The kitchen is fully equipped with a microwave, refrigerator, electric kettle, cookware, and dining sets for two.

Is smoking allowed?

Clars is a non-smoking apartment. We ask guests to smoke outside the unit only.

03 / Booking

Reservations & Payment

How do I confirm a reservation?

Send us your preferred dates and guest count using the booking form or WhatsApp. Our team will confirm availability and share payment details.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer or cash. Full payment confirms your reservation, and a receipt is issued for every payment.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a charge. Please confirm the terms for your dates with our team.

Is there a discount for longer stays?

Yes. Stays of 7 nights or more qualify for our reduced Long Stay rate of ₦135,000 per night. Contact our team for a custom quote.

Do rates change during peak periods?

Rates may vary during public holidays and peak periods. Please confirm the rate for your specific dates when you enquire.

Can I request an airport pickup?

Yes — private transfers from Port Harcourt International can be arranged as an optional extra. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll share details.

04 / The Apartment

Amenities & Comfort

Is there 24/7 power?

Yes. The apartment runs on an automatic switchover system with silent standby generators on site, so power is uninterrupted day and night.

How fast is the Wi-Fi?

Our fibre broadband is fast enough for 4K streaming and video calls all day. Remote work is comfortably supported.

What's on the Smart TV?

A large 4K Smart TV pre-configured with Netflix, YouTube, and DSTV — ready to go when you arrive.

Is housekeeping included?

Yes. Regular cleaning, fresh bath linens, and pristine room preparation are included in every stay. Extra housekeeping can be arranged on request.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes. The apartment is air-conditioned throughout, with units in the living area and bedroom.

Are bath towels provided?

Yes. Fresh bath and hand towels are provided for all guests, and refreshed during your stay.

05 / Location

Getting There & Safety

Where exactly is Clars located?

Clars Apartment sits inside a private gated compound on Tombia Extension, GRA Phase 3, Port Harcourt, Rivers State. Exact arrival instructions are shared once your booking is confirmed.

How far is the airport?

Port Harcourt International Airport is a comfortable drive away. We can arrange a private transfer on request.

Is the area safe?

GRA Phase 3 is one of Port Harcourt's most established and peaceful residential districts. The apartment is inside a gated compound with 24-hour security guards and CCTV.

What's nearby?

GRA offices and banks, restaurants and lounges, supermarkets, and shopping are all within easy reach. Our team is happy to share recommendations.

Can I get a receipt for my stay?

Yes. A receipt is issued for every payment. Let us know if you need it addressed to a company.

How do I get in touch during my stay?

Our reservation desk is reachable by phone or WhatsApp 24/7. You'll have the direct number before you arrive.
