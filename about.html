01 / Our Story

A quiet idea
of home.

Clars Apartment was born from a simple observation: Port Harcourt deserved a short-stay experience that felt less like a hotel and more like a thoughtfully curated home. A place where the power never blinks, the Wi-Fi never falters, and the design invites you to exhale.

Tucked inside a private gated compound in Tombia Extension, GRA Phase 3, our apartment is a deliberate contrast to the city's rush — a sanctuary of warm neutrals, natural textures, and considered comfort.

GRA Phase 3, Port Harcourt Private Gated Compound 5.0 Guest Rating
Our Philosophy

"Luxury isn't loud. It's the quiet confidence of a space that works perfectly — the generator that hums softly, the sheets that feel fresh, the gate that keeps the world at bay."

02 / Design Ethos

Warm Minimalism,
Considered Detail

Every material, every finish, every fixture chosen with intention.

A Palette of Calm

Dust grey neutrals, olive accents, and rich walnut wood create a grounding, earthy atmosphere. The tones are deliberately muted — nothing competes for your attention.

Comfort You Can Feel

From the weight of the bath linens to the ergonomics of the workspace, every touchpoint is designed for ease. The apartment should feel like it's been waiting for you.

Nature at the Edges

Lush greenery accents bring the outside in, softening the modern lines and connecting the interior to Port Harcourt's tropical warmth.
