04 / Before You Book

Quick Answers

How quickly will you respond?

We aim to respond to every enquiry within a few hours during the day, and by the next morning for late-night messages.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can often be arranged — just mention it in your enquiry.

Is my reservation confirmed immediately?

Your dates are held once we confirm availability on WhatsApp. The reservation is fully confirmed once payment is received, and a receipt is issued.

What if my plans change?

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in. Within 48 hours, a charge may apply. Let us know as early as possible and we'll do our best to help.

Can I request an airport pickup?

Yes — private transfers from Port Harcourt International can be arranged as an optional extra. Mention it in your enquiry and we'll share details.

Do you accept cash?

Yes. We accept bank transfer or cash. Full payment is required to confirm the reservation, and a receipt is provided for every payment.
